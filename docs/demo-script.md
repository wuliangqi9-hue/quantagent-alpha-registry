# Demo Script

Target length: 2-3 minutes.

## Opening

QuantAgent Alpha Registry turns crypto factor research into an explainable AI
strategy selector with an auditable decision trail on Mantle.

## Scene 1: Select Asset

Show the dashboard and select BTC, ETH, or SOL.

Narration:

The agent can run in live-data mode, but this demo also has an offline fallback
so the evaluation flow is stable even if market APIs rate-limit.

## Scene 2: Factor View

Show the factor radar or bar chart.

Narration:

The agent is not guessing from a prompt. It computes market and derivative
factors, then turns them into a compact decision report.

## Scene 3: Strategy Selection

Show market regime, selected strategy, confidence, and risk warnings.

Narration:

The selector reuses prior QuantAgent and Hummingbot experiments. It chooses a
strategy based on regime, factor drivers, and benchmark evidence.

## Scene 4: Benchmark Evidence

Show historical chart and signal markers.

Narration:

The benchmark is evidence for the workflow, not a promise of profit. The app
keeps the limitations visible: sample size, slippage, and regime shift risk.

## Scene 5: Mantle Proof

Click record signal or show the already-recorded signal.

Narration:

The full decision report stays off-chain, but its hash and key metadata are
recorded on Mantle. This creates a public audit trail for the agent's decisions.

## Closing

QuantAgent bridges quantitative research and Web3 transparency: factors and
strategy reasoning off-chain, proof and accountability on-chain.
