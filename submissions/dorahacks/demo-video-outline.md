# Demo Video Outline

Target length: 2-3 minutes.

## 0:00-0:15 Opening

Show the dashboard.

Script:

QuantAgent Alpha Registry turns crypto factor research into an explainable AI
strategy selector with a public Mantle decision trail.

## 0:15-0:45 Analyze Asset

Select BTC, ETH, or SOL and click Analyze.

Script:

The system can use live market data, but it also has offline sample snapshots so
the judge demo stays reliable if external APIs fail.

## 0:45-1:15 Factor View

Show factor radar and bar chart.

Script:

The agent is not answering from a prompt alone. It computes standardized market
and derivative factors, then uses them as the basis for strategy selection.

## 1:15-1:45 Strategy Decision

Show regime, selected strategy, confidence, and drivers.

Script:

The selector maps market regime and factor drivers to a transparent strategy
choice. This version uses SuperTrend, Bollinger, and MACD plus Bollinger as the
initial candidate pool.

## 1:45-2:15 Evidence and Risk

Show benchmark chart and caveats.

Script:

Benchmarks are workflow evidence, not a promise of profit. The UI keeps
limitations visible: slippage, fees, sample size, and regime shifts.

## 2:15-2:45 Mantle Proof

Click Record on Mantle or show the recorded proof panel.

Script:

The full decision report stays off-chain, but its hash and metadata are recorded
on Mantle. That creates an auditable trail for agent decisions.

## 2:45-3:00 Close

Script:

QuantAgent bridges quant research and Web3 transparency: factors and reasoning
off-chain, accountability on Mantle.
